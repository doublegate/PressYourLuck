## Show History & Background

### Broadcast Details
- **Network**: CBS
- **Premiere**: September 19, 1983, 10:30 AM ET (9:30 CT/MT/PT)
- **Finale**: September 26, 1986
- **Total Episodes**: 758
- **Replaced**: Child's Play
- **Tape Location**: CBS Television City, 7800 Beverly Boulevard, Fairfax, Los Angeles, California (Studios 33, 41, 43)

### Show Format
- Contestants answer trivia questions to earn "spins"
- Spins used on the Big Board - 18 perimeter squares cycling through prizes
- Landing on Whammy eliminates accumulated winnings and plays animation
- 4 Whammies = elimination ("Whammied Out")
- Two-round format: Question Round + Board Round per round

### Creation
- **Creators**: Bill Carruthers and Jan McCormack
- **Network Development**: CBS Daytime
- **Original Concept**: Evolution of earlier game show mechanics with added Whammy character

## Key Personnel

### Peter Tomarken (Host)
- Full name: Peter Alan Tomarken
- Born: December 7, 1942, Olean, New York
- Died: March 13, 2006 (plane crash during Angel Flight volunteer mission)
- **Hosting Style**: Warm, energetic, genuinely excited for contestants
- Previous work: Hit Man (with Rod Roddy)
- Known phrases: "Big Bucks! No Whammies!", "Stop!"

### Rod Roddy (Announcer)
- Full name: Robert Ray Roddy
- Born: September 28, 1937, Fort Worth, Texas
- Died: October 27, 2003
- **Announcing Style**: Enthusiastic, distinctive voice
- Later role: The Price Is Right announcer (1986-2003)
- Fill-in announcers: John Harlan, Charlie O'Donnell

### Bill Carruthers (Creator/Director/Whammy Voice)
- Multi-role: Created show, directed, voiced all Whammy animations
- Selected Savage Steve Holland personally for animation design
- Voice style: High-pitched, mischievous, taunting

### Savage Steve Holland (Animator)
- Full name: Steven Holland
- Born: 1958
- Education: California Institute of the Arts (animation)
- **Design Origin**: Sketched original Whammy on a napkin; Carruthers approved immediately
- Animation Technology: Early computer animation system (Holland called it "steam-powered")
- Later career: Director of "Better Off Dead" (1985), "One Crazy Summer" (1986)

### Bill Kopp (Co-Animator)
- Worked with Holland on Whammy animations
- Additional animation and design support

## Board Mechanics & Patterns

### Board Layout
- **Shape**: 18 squares forming perimeter of 6x5 grid
- **Active Squares**: Only perimeter (corners + edges)
- **Center**: Whammy animation display area
- **Movement**: Clockwise cycling

### Square Numbering (Clockwise from Top-Left)
```
[ 0] [ 1] [ 2] [ 3] [ 4] [ 5]
[17]                    [ 6]
[16]      CENTER        [ 7]
[15]                    [ 8]
[14] [13] [12] [11] [10] [ 9]
```

### Prize Cycling
- Each square cycles through 3 different prizes
- Cycling speed increases during active play
- Synchronized but offset patterns

### Michael Larson Discovery (June 8, 1984)
- **Method**: VHS frame-by-frame analysis over 6 months
- **Finding**: Board used only 5 looping patterns (not random)
- **Key Squares**: 4 and 8 NEVER contained Whammy
- **Additional**: Squares 4 and 8 always included +Spin in Round 2
- **Result**: 47 spins, $110,237 in cash and prizes
- **Aftermath**: Board reprogrammed with 32 patterns (August 1984)

### Post-Larson Patterns
- **Pattern Count**: 32 unique patterns
- **Whammy Distribution**: More evenly distributed
- **Implementation Date**: Episodes after August 1984

### Special Square Rules
- **Pick a Corner**: Always in square #6 during Round 2
- **Big Bucks**: Always in square #12, awards amount from square #4
  - Round 1: $1,000-$1,250
  - Round 2: $5,000+
- **Most Specials**: Removed after being hit once (except Big Bucks, Pick a Corner)

## Set Design & Visual Style

### CBS Television City Studio
- **Primary Studio**: Studio 33 (also used 41, 43)
- **Era Aesthetic**: Mid-1980s game show
- **Lighting**: Bright, colorful, theatrical

### Color Palette (Authentic CRT Television)
- **Background**: Deep purple (CRT black level)
- **Primary**: Gold (#FFD700) - highlights, borders
- **Secondary**: Hot Pink (#FF1493) - accents
- **Accent**: Electric Blue (#00BFFF) - neon effects
- **Whammy Red**: Vibrant red for Whammy character

### Board Visual Elements
- **Square Colors**:
  - Cash: Green backgrounds
  - Prizes: Blue backgrounds
  - Special: Gold/Yellow backgrounds
  - Whammy: Red backgrounds with character
- **Text**: Bold, high-contrast for TV visibility
- **Effects**: Neon glow, rainbow shimmer, chase lights

### Contestant Podiums
- **Count**: 3 podiums
- **Display**: Name, score, Whammy count
- **Active Indicator**: Highlighted when current player

### Chase Lights
- **Location**: Around board perimeter
- **Count**: ~48 bulbs
- **Pattern**: Sequential chasing, faster during spins
- **Color**: Warm incandescent yellow-white

## Technical Implementation References

### Sprite Animation in ggez
- **Approach**: Sprite sheets with atlas mapping
- **Frame Rate**: Match original (29.97 fps) or smooth 60 fps
- **Optimization**: SpriteBatch for bulk rendering

### Cell Shading Techniques
- **Method**: Flat colors with hard edge outlines
- **Implementation**: Either in sprite artwork or via shader
- **Key**: Limited palette, no gradients (matches Holland style)

### CRT Shader Effects
- **Scanlines**: Horizontal dark lines
- **Phosphor Bloom**: Glow around bright elements
- **Barrel Distortion**: Slight screen curvature
- **Chromatic Aberration**: RGB color fringing
- **Vignette**: Darkened corners
- **WGSL Reference**: https://wgsl-shader-depot.vercel.app/samples/complexCRT

### Audio Implementation
- **Format**: WAV/OGG for sound effects, OGG for music
- **Library**: rodio (via ggez)
- **Sample Rate**: 44,100 Hz (CD quality)
